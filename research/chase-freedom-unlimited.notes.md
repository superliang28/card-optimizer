## What was verified (official source in parentheses)
- **Name/network/fee**: Chase Freedom Unlimited, Visa (Platinum or Signature), $0 annual fee (card page; P&T).
- **Foreign transaction fee 3%** (P&T LGC61498). Re-confirmed 2026-09-22 by Doctor of Credit: "The Freedom
  Unlimited still has a foreign transaction fee" after the Flex dropped its FTF on 2026-09-21. The CFU card
  page has no "no foreign transaction fee" language (the Flex page does).
- **Earning** (RPA0509 verbatim): 1.5% on all purchases (= 1.5 points/$); 3% total on "drugstores and dining at
  restaurants including takeout and eligible delivery services"; 5% total on "purchases made using your card
  through Chase Travel"; portion paid with points earns nothing; FROSCH/Valerie Wilson bookings excluded.
  Cash Back is tracked as points: $1 Cash Back = 100 points.
- **Category definitions/exclusions** (chase.com/RewardsCategoryFAQs, which the agreement points to):
  - Restaurants: primary business sit-down/eat-in dining incl. fast food and fine dining; delivery/takeout
    services qualify if coded as restaurants. Excluded: merchants inside stadiums, hotels, casinos, theme parks,
    grocery stores, department stores; bakeries; caterers; meal-kit delivery; gift-card merchants.
  - Drugstores: specialize in Rx and OTC medicines, supplements, health items (may also sell cosmetics,
    toiletries, household goods). Excluded: warehouse clubs, discount stores, grocery stores, any general
    merchant that merely has an onsite pharmacy.
  - Chase Travel: prepaid travel on chasetravel.com or by phone — airfare, hotels, car rentals, cruises,
    activities, tours. Excluded: FROSCH etc., and any portion paid with rewards.
  - Digital wallets/third-party payment accounts earn a category bonus only if they pass the category.
- **Lyft**: card-page footnote: "2% cash back total on qualifying Lyft products and services purchased through the
  Lyft mobile application through 09/30/2027 … 0.5% additional." Qualifying: rideshare, bike/scooter rides,
  subscription/membership products. Excluded: gift cards, car rentals, vehicle service centers, misc fees.
  lyft.com/chase matches. Doctor of Credit (2025-03-31): 5x ended 2025-03-31, 2% from 2025-04-01. **The 5%
  Lyft bonus is NOT active.**
- **DoorDash** (Chase terms page): 6 months complimentary DashPass on first activation between 02/01/2025 and
  12/31/2027 with the Chase card as default payment; then auto-enrolled at the then-current monthly rate.
  $10 off one qualifying non-restaurant order each calendar quarter through 12/31/2027 (subtotal only; forfeits
  at quarter end; must remain enrolled in DashPass; must pay with the enrolled Chase card, not Apple/Google Pay;
  US address; one redemption per quarter per person). Card page footnote agrees ("up to $10 off quarterly on
  non-restaurant DoorDash orders through 12/31/2027"; activate by 12/31/2027).
- **Instacart**: NO current benefit. The Freedom Instacart benefit (3 months Instacart+ plus up to $10/quarter)
  ended 7/31/2024 (Frequent Miler 2024-07-26). The May 2025 Chase–Instacart program (through 12/31/2027) covers
  co-brands ($10/mo) and Ink Cash/Unlimited ($20/mo) only — Freedom cards are not listed (Doctor of Credit
  2025-05-02; instacart.com/p/chase-cobrands; Instacart help center). The CFU card page shows no Instacart benefit.
- **Redemption** (RPA0509): 1 point = $0.01 for cash (statement credit or deposit to eligible US Chase account),
  gift cards and Chase Travel; other options may be worth more or less; no minimum for cash (card page); points
  never expire while the account is open; 30 days after voluntary closure; immediate forfeiture on closure for
  misuse/fraud/bankruptcy; redeemed as statement credit on death.
- **Combine points — the exact rule** (RPA0509, section "Combine points with other Chase cards with Ultimate
  Rewards"): "You can move your points, but only to another Chase card with Ultimate Rewards belonging to you or
  one member of your household. You can't move points to another eligible card if either account is prohibited
  from earning or using points at the time you attempt to move the points. Go to chase.com/ultimaterewards,
  call the number on the back of your card, or visit a Chase branch to move your points."
  Chase education page (how-to-combine-chase-credit-card-points-in-household): household member = someone who
  "shares your address" — "spouse, partner, roommate or any family member you live with"; "You can only combine
  your points with one person"; steps: log in → Ultimate Rewards → Combine Points → choose source card → enter
  recipient's information → confirm.
  Secondary (TPG updated 2026-07-02; AwardWallet 2026-09-07): first-time linking of another person's account
  requires calling the number on the back of the card; afterwards the linked card appears online; transfers are
  instant. App path: Chase app → Ultimate Rewards → pick card → Combine Points → source → destination → amount
  → Review → Submit.
- **What combined points are worth in the spouse's Sapphire Preferred** (this is where the "1.25 cents" premise
  needs correcting):
  - Partner transfers 1:1 to 14 programs (CSP page lists Aer Lingus, Aeroplan, British Airways, Flying Blue,
    Iberia, JetBlue, Singapore KrisFlyer, Southwest, United, Virgin Atlantic, IHG, Marriott, Hyatt, Wyndham).
  - Chase Travel: base 1 cent; Points Boost up to 1.5¢ (hotels/economy) and 1.75¢ (premium cabins) on select
    bookings (TPG 2025-07-15 and 2026-09-21; Doctor of Credit; Upgraded Points 2026-06-15).
  - Legacy flat 1.25¢: only for CSP accounts opened/applied/upgraded before 2025-06-23, only on points that were
    in the account (earned OR combined in) by 2025-10-25, redeemable through 2027-10-26; points are consumed
    first-in-first-out (Frequent Miler 2025-06-17 and 2025-10-25; Doctor of Credit). Points combined on/after
    2025-10-26 are "only eligible for Points Boost" (Frequent Miler 2025-10-25).
  - I could not find a Chase-hosted Points Boost FAQ page (two URL guesses 404'd; search budget exhausted), so
    the transition rules rest on consistent secondary reporting that quotes Chase's FAQ.
- **Protections** (all from the Guide to Benefits, effective 10/1/2024):
  - Auto Rental Coverage: up to $60,000; secondary in US (primary if no personal auto insurance); primary outside
    US; ≤31 consecutive days; decline CDW/LDW; exclusions as listed in JSON; report within 100 days.
  - Extended Warranty: +1 year on manufacturer US warranties ≤3 years (cumulative with store/service contracts);
    never beyond 4 years total; $10,000/item, $50,000/account.
  - Purchase Protection: 120 days (90 NY); $500/item; $50,000/account; secondary.
  - Roadside Assistance: pay-per-use (fee quoted on call); US & Canada; tow 5 mi, tire, battery, lockout,
    fuel 5 gal, winching; light-duty ≤10,000 lb. (Chase marketing calls this "Roadside Dispatch".)
  - Travel and Emergency Assistance: referrals/coordination only; cardholder pays all costs.
  - Trip Cancellation/Interruption: $1,500 per covered traveler, $6,000 per trip; common-carrier fares only;
    trips ≤60 days; secondary; covered reasons/exclusions as in JSON. Card page and Chase education page agree.
  - NOT in the guide: trip delay, baggage delay, lost luggage reimbursement, travel accident, cell phone,
    return protection, price protection.
- **Costco**: customerservice.costco.com a_id/719: US warehouses and gas stations accept "All Visa Cards";
  Mastercard only on Costco.com.
- **Welcome bonus (standard)**: $200 after $500 in 3 months; 24-month rule (card page).

## Conflicts between sources (official wins)
- Frequent Miler's CFU review (2026-03-12) describes DoorDash as "$10 monthly" and "3 months" DashPass; the
  Chase terms page and card page say 6 months and $10 **quarterly**. Used official.
- DoorDash help-center page (last updated 2025-06-23) still shows the pre-2025 Freedom structure
  ("free 3 months, half off 9 months, activate by 1/31/25"). Outdated; Chase terms used.
- Upgraded Points (2026-06-15) listed CFU as "1 cent per point (no boost)"; Chase's 2026-09-21 release and TPG
  (2026-09-21) say Freedom cards now get hotel Points Boost up to 10% more. Treated as a September 2026 change;
  flagged `verified: false` because the CFU product page/agreement do not mention it yet.
- APR: card page fetch reported "18.24%–29.99%" while P&T (12/16/2025) says 18.24%–27.74% (29.99% is the
  penalty APR). Not in schema; noted only.
- Chase's household page implies you can enter a recipient's info online; TPG/AwardWallet say the first link
  to another person's account requires a phone call. Both recorded; flagged as partially verified.
