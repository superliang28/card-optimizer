## Ambiguities / conflicts noted
- Chase FAQ says 15,000-point top-up; Chase cardmember page, Marriott FNA page and TPG say 25,000 (since 3/12/2026). Used 25,000.
- Upgraded Points news said airline credit "only available for 2026"; later sources and the Chase page show a new-cardmember extension to 6/30/2027. Both are right for their cohorts; JSON records both.
- hellosafe baggage delay "3 days" vs. official "up to 5 days". Used official.
- "Up to 17X" marketing vs. RPA 6X: RPA makes clear only 6 points come from the card; the rest are Bonvoy base/elite points and vary by brand (5-point brands → up to 12X total at Silver).
- Cap period: schema only allows calendar-year/cardmember-year/etc.; RPA's definition is December-statement-to-December-statement, recorded as "calendar-year" with explanation.
