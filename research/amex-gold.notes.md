# American Express Gold Card (US consumer) -- research notes

Card id: `amex-gold` | Data as of: 2026-09-29 | Researcher: Claude (Fable 5.1), ~45 fetches/searches

## Headline state (September 2026)
- Annual fee **$325** (Amex Credit Intel page, "Last updated July 17, 2026"). Up to 5 Additional Cards free, $35 each after.
- Foreign transaction fee **0%** (official card page).
- Earning (official card page, Sept 2026): **4X restaurants worldwide** (cap $50,000/calendar year, then 1X); **4X U.S. supermarkets** (cap $25,000/calendar year, then 1X); **5X prepaid hotels** via AmexTravel.com / Amex Travel App (raised from 2X on Apr 30, 2026); **3X flights** direct or via Amex Travel; **2X prepaid car rentals via Amex Travel and cruises booked & paid via AmexTravel.com**; **1X** everything else.
- Credits: $10/mo Uber Cash ($120), $10/mo Dining ($120; Grubhub incl. Seamless, Cheesecake Factory, Five Guys, Buffalo Wild Wings, Wonder), $50 semiannual Resy ($100), $7/mo Dunkin' ($84), Hotel Collection $100 per 2+ night Amex Travel stay, plus a limited-time one-time $96 Uber One annual-membership credit through Oct 30, 2026.
- New in 2026: Hertz Five Star status (enrollment required).
- Protections: Purchase Protection $10k/item, $50k/yr, 90 days; Extended Warranty +1 yr on warranties <=5 yrs, $10k/$50k; Baggage $1,250 carry-on / $500 checked (high-risk items $250; NY $10k aggregate); Car Rental secondary up to $50,000, 30 days, excludes AU/IT/NZ; Trip Delay $300/trip after 12+ hours, 2 claims/12 months. No cell phone, no return protection, no trip cancellation, no roadside; Global Assist Hotline yes.
