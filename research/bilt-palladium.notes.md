## The housing mechanism, precisely (Flexible Bilt Cash option — this cardholder)

1. Everyday Spend (all Eligible Purchases except Bilt Housing Payments, including authorized-user spend) earns **2 points per $1 plus 4% Bilt Cash**.
2. Housing payments (rent/mortgage/HOA/property tax/etc. paid through Bilt) earn **0 points and 0 Bilt Cash automatically**. There is **no transaction fee**.
3. At the moment each housing payment is made, the cardholder chooses how much Bilt Cash to redeem. Conversion: **$3 Bilt Cash = 100 points, i.e. $0.03 per point, $30 per 1,000 points**; minimum $0.03 (1 point). Cap: **1 point per $1 of that payment**. Any Bilt Cash in the balance can be used (prior months, the $200 annual, the $300 welcome, status-earned Bilt Cash). It cannot be applied after the fact to an earlier payment.
4. No monthly/annual cap on housing points other than the 1X-per-payment cap. No 250-point floor and no 1.25X tier under this option.
5. Unused Bilt Cash stays in the balance for other redemptions (dollar-for-dollar merchant credits with monthly caps, Point Accelerator, hotel bookings, transfer-bonus buy-ups). It **cannot** be cashed out or used as a statement credit, and expires Dec 31 of the year earned except $100 carried over (FIFO).
6. Switching to Housing-only (0–1.25X by Everyday Spend Ratio, 250-point floor, no Bilt Cash) takes effect the first day of the next billing cycle.

## Worked example: $5,000/month rent, Flexible Bilt Cash

Let X = everyday (non-housing) spend in the month.

- Points from everyday spend: **2·X**
- Bilt Cash earned: **0.04·X**
- Bilt Cash needed to fully unlock 1X on $5,000 rent: **5,000 × $0.03 = $150/month** ($1,800/yr)
- Everyday spend needed to generate $150 in a month: **$150 / 0.04 = $3,750**
- Points if fully unlocked: **5,000/month** (60,000/yr — enough for Silver status on its own)

| Everyday spend X | Points on spend | Bilt Cash earned | Bilt Cash to rent | Rent points | Leftover Bilt Cash | Total points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $2,000 | 4,000 | $80 | $80 | 2,667 | $0 | 6,667 |
| $3,000 | 6,000 | $120 | $120 | 4,000 | $0 | 10,000 |
| $3,750 | 7,500 | $150 | $150 | 5,000 (max) | $0 | 12,500 |
| $5,000 | 10,000 | $200 | $150 | 5,000 (max) | $50 | 15,000 |
| $7,500 | 15,000 | $300 | $150 | 5,000 (max) | $150 | 20,000 |

Plus fixed inflows: $200 annual Bilt Cash (Jan 1) = 6,667 rent points if converted; $300 welcome Bilt Cash (year 1) = 10,000; $50 Bilt Cash per 25,000 Qualifying Points earned (~$120–$240/yr at these volumes).

Effective rate when Bilt Cash is routed to rent: 4% × (1/$0.03) = **1.33 extra points per everyday dollar**, i.e. the Palladium is effectively a 3.33X card on everyday spend up to the point where rent is fully unlocked ($3,750 spend for $5,000 rent), then 2X + 4% Bilt Cash for other outlets beyond that.

### Break-even analysis for the tool

- Housing unlock buys points at **3.0¢ each**. Spending the same Bilt Cash dollar-for-dollar on Lyft/Walgreens/Grubhub/Bilt Travel/fitness returns face value ($1) but with small monthly caps ($10/$10/$10/$50–$100/$40).
- Rent unlock beats merchant credits only if the user values a Bilt point above 3.0¢. At TPG 2.2¢, $150 -> 5,000 pts ≈ $110 vs $150 face. At 1.6¢ (USCCG) ≈ $80.
- Point Accelerator buys points at **4.0¢** ($200 -> 5,000 pts), strictly worse than housing unlock (3.0¢) unless rent is already fully unlocked.
- Housing-only comparison at $5,000 rent: 25%/$1,250 -> 2,500 pts; 50%/$2,500 -> 3,750; 75%/$3,750 -> 5,000; 100%/$5,000 -> 6,250; <25% -> 250. At $3,750 spend both options give 5,000 rent points, but Flexible also has no Bilt Cash left over, so they tie; at $5,000 spend Housing-only gives 1,250 more rent points while Flexible gives $50 of Bilt Cash instead (4.0¢/pt break-even). Below $3,750 spend, Flexible strictly dominates (Housing-only steps down to 3,750/2,500/250 while Flexible scales linearly at 33.3 pts per Bilt Cash dollar), and Flexible is the only option that permits Point Accelerator and merchant credits.

## Ambiguities worth surfacing in the tool

- Under Flexible, "1X on rent" is a ceiling, not an automatic rate; the tool should compute rent points = min(BiltCashRedeemed / 0.03, rentAmount).
- The Everyday Spend Ratio uses posting dates within the *billing cycle*, not the calendar month; the tool can approximate with calendar months but should note the timing risk near cycle boundaries.
- Bilt Cash earned in December but unspent is at risk beyond the $100 rollover; the tool should treat Bilt Cash as expiring annually, not as cash.
- The $200 annual Bilt Cash and $50-per-25k-points Bilt Cash are real inflows that should be modeled as convertible to 6,667 and 1,667 rent points respectively (or to merchant credits at face value).
- JSON contains three non-schema top-level keys added deliberately for the tool: `housingRewards`, `biltCash`, `welcomeOffer` (plus `issuerLegal`, `networkTier`, `authorizedUserFee`, `priceProtection`, and `biltCashPercent` on the `everything` earning entry).
