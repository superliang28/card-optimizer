# Prime Visa (Chase / Amazon) — research notes

Card id: `amazon-prime-visa` · Data as of 2026-09-29 · Cardholder assumed to have an active, eligible Prime membership.

## Bottom line

- 5% back (with eligible Prime) at Amazon.com, Whole Foods Market, Amazon Fresh, Audible.com, Shopbop, Twitch/Woot via Amazon Pay, and on prepaid Chase Travel bookings; 2% at gas stations, restaurants, and local transit & commuting (incl. rideshare); 1% elsewhere. Unlimited, no caps.
- $0 annual fee, no foreign transaction fee. Rewards are "% back" tracked as points, 100 points = $1, fixed 1 cent/point on Amazon checkout, cash, gift cards, and Chase Travel. Not Ultimate Rewards (no transfers, no pooling).
- Protections come from Chase Guide to Benefits BGC11359 (effective 10/01/24): rental CDW is SECONDARY in the US (primary abroad), Purchase Protection $500/item, Extended Warranty +1 yr, Baggage Delay $100/day x3, Lost Luggage $3,000, Travel Accident $500k, pay-per-use Roadside, Travel & Emergency Assistance referrals. No trip cancellation/delay, no cell phone, no return protection.
- If Prime lapses the card stays open but Amazon/Whole Foods/Chase Travel earning drops to 3% (identical to the non-Prime Amazon Visa); Prime Card Bonus and delivery-option bonuses stop.

## Verified vs. not verified

Verified from official sources: all earning rates and category definitions; all exclusions listed in the JSON; redemption value (1 cent) and mechanics; no caps; points expiration rules; Prime-lapse behaviour (drops to 3%, card stays open); annual fee $0; FX fee none; APRs; every protection limit and the absence of trip cancellation/delay, cell phone, return protection; Prime Card Bonus terms; delivery-option bonus terms; Costco Visa acceptance.

Not verified / ambiguous (flagged in JSON):
1. Whether Audible.com newly began earning 5% in mid-2026 or was simply newly advertised. Wayback dates the marketing change to between 2026-06-08 and 2026-07-12; no press release or blog post found announcing it. Amazon Fresh is unchanged (still 5%).
2. Cash-back redemption minimum at Chase.com. Amazon help says minimums apply to gift cards and cash back at Chase.com; the RPA only says minimums "may apply"; Bankrate says none; CNBC says gift cards are $25 increments. Shop with Points at Amazon has no minimum (confirmed).
3. Amazon Business (business.amazon.com) purchases: not listed as participating or excluded on amazon.com/primevisa/benefits. Assume not guaranteed to earn 5%.
4. Visa Signature concierge / Luxury Hotel Collection: secondary sources only; not on any Chase/Amazon page; the Guide covers both Visa Signature and Visa Platinum, and Platinum accounts would not get Signature network perks. Marked verified: false.
5. The Q3 2026 targeted 5–9% grocery/gas/restaurant promotion: Kudos only; targeted, so the cardholder may not have it. Marked verified: false in recentChanges.
6. Items excluded from Shop with Points (Bankrate says some digital content): Amazon's "Shop with Points Restrictions" help sub-page was not read; not load-bearing for earning decisions.
7. Costco Gas earning 2%: Costco fuel is generally coded as a gas station, but Chase does not guarantee merchant coding; stated cautiously.

## Conflicts noted

- Secondary sources (TPG, Upgraded Points, Bankrate, Dailydrop) still headline "Amazon Fresh" in the 5% list; Chase and Amazon now headline "Audible.com". Official fine print includes both, so both are in the JSON.
- Doctor of Credit lists "2% at restaurants, gas stations, and drugstores"; official terms say gas stations, local transit and commuting, restaurants. Official wins.
- Bankrate "no minimum" for cash back vs. Amazon help "minimum redemption amounts apply ... at Chase.com". Flagged rather than resolved.

## Schema notes

- `currency.id` set to `cash` with `unit: percent` because the program markets % back with a fixed 1-cent point and is not Chase Ultimate Rewards.
- `rideshare` included as its own row because the issuer's headline explicitly says "including rideshare", even though Uber/Lyft are not named individually; noted in that row.
- `credits` is empty: the card has no statement credits. The new-cardmember gift card is listed under `perks`.
- `rotating` omitted (Discover-only per spec); the Prime Card Bonus is captured in `merchantRates` and `perks`.
- `purchaseProtection.perYear` = 50000 reflects the Guide's "$50,000 per Account" cap (not strictly per year); noted in the object.
- Search budget: 200 WebSearch calls were exhausted late in the session; final confirmations were done with direct fetches (WebFetch/curl). Total fetches/searches well above the 10 required.
